Balance point is built into the settings, using local weather data by zip code.

C-Wire Adapter (CWA)

Creates a C wire from the Y and G wires. Not for dual-transformer or heat-only systems. A spare wire at the stat can often be used as C instead.

Power

24V AC, up to 2A output. Not for line-voltage (110/120V) or millivolt systems.

Straight answer: communicating systems

Nuve doesn't run proprietary communicating equipment yet. Most of your base is conventional 24V, and that's where Nuve goes.

Add-on for Aire Serv

Catch the leak before the callback.

  • Wireless water sensors at the drain pan, water heater and under sinks
  • A leak flashes an alert on the stat telling the homeowner to call Aire Serv, and your team gets it too
  • Temp / humidity sensors even out hot rooms, which matters in Houston humidity
Tech tip on placement: put temp/humidity sensors in bedrooms and living areas, about 5 ft up on an interior wall, within 50 ft of the stat. Keep them out of direct sun, kitchens and bathrooms, and away from exterior walls and vents.
