Whole-home protection

Thermostats + water sensors.

This is where All Set stands apart from HVAC-only shops. The newer Nuve thermostats pair with wireless water leak sensors for the drain pan, under sinks and by the water heater. If one picks up a leak, the thermostat tells the homeowner to call All Set and your team gets alerted at the same time.

  • A plumbing call before the floodCatch the leak while it's a repair, not a restoration job.
  • A reason to sell protection, not a gadget"All Set watches your home 24/7" is an easy story for your techs to tell.
Coming soon: Nuve Flow

Shut off the main from anywhere.

The Nuve Flow remote main water valve installs on the main line and comes in the same Home Protection Hub as the thermostat and leak sensors. When a sensor picks up a leak, the water can be shut off at the main remotely, before it turns into a flooded floor. Every install is plumbing work for All Set, and every home it protects runs on your logo.
