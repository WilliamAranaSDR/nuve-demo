How to market it

The Akian launch plan.

You don't need a new marketing budget. You already have the customers, the techs in homes every day, and the CRM. Here's how to put the thermostat to work.

Week 1

Brand it "Akian Home Protection"

Your logo on the stat and your app. Sell it as protection for the home, thermostat plus leak sensors, not as a smart gadget.

Week 1-2

Put it on every truck

Techs offer it on every heating, cooling and plumbing call, and it goes in with every install. Every unit on a wall is a customer who calls Akian first.

Week 2-4

Build a membership around it

Bundle the thermostat, leak monitoring and seasonal tune-ups into one plan. Tie it to the membership in ServiceTitan so reminders run on their own. Norman Air is a Nuve partner doing exactly this.

Month 1

Market it to your customer list

Email and text past customers from ServiceTitan: "Protect your home with Akian." Your existing list is the cheapest lead you'll ever buy.

Every season

Message the wall, test the systems

Send tune-up reminders straight to the thermostat screen, then run performance tests and call the homes that need attention. Shoulder seasons fill themselves.
